Turn 2 · planning first, inputs always visible
Set it up once, see the week
The existing site asks for span, date, time, boat height and margin down five sections, then makes you press Check Now or Find Times. Here every input sits in one panel that never scrolls away, there is no submit button — the week redraws as you change anything — and the whole run of days is on screen instead of one moment.
2A Setup on top, week below
Every input is a tap or a stepper — no keyboard, no calendar modal. Each day lists its windows as plain start and end times, tabular-aligned so they scan down the column. Date and time lead the setup panel, with the answer for that exact moment directly under the inputs; span, margin and boat height sit below at equal weight. Days can carry three windows — Friday splits around a weak high tide. Safe, caution and danger keep the current calculator’s thresholds: a window is caution at its shoulders and safe once there is 0.5 m spare. LINZ fetch status comes before the times. The screen scrolls; the setup card stays pinned.
